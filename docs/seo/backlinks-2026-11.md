understanding of the business (NAP consistency), not just referral traffic.

## 1. Google Business Profile

- **Sign-up:** https://www.google.com/business/
- **Suggested category:** Appliance repair service (primary). Secondary: Refrigerator repair service, Washer & dryer repair service.
- **Free:** yes.
- **What it asks for:** business name, address (or a service area if you do not take walk-ins), phone, website, hours, categories, service list, photos, and a postcard or phone verification.
- **Why first:** this is the single highest-value listing for local search, and it is the one that drives the map pack. The profile already exists — see the TODO below.

## 2. Better Business Bureau (BBB) — Greater Houston

- **Sign-up:** https://www.bbb.org/get-listed
- **Suggested category:** Major Appliance Repair.
- **Free:** a basic listing is free; BBB Accreditation is paid (annual fee based on company size).
- **What it asks for:** legal business name, physical address, phone, website, year established, number of employees, owner name, and business licence details if applicable.
- **Why:** strong trust signal for homeowners booking a technician for the first time, and the profile ranks for "<company name> reviews" searches.

## 3. Yelp for Business

- **Sign-up:** https://biz.yelp.com/
- **Suggested category:** Home Services > Appliances & Repair.
- **Free:** yes for a claimed listing; advertising is optional and paid.
- **What it asks for:** business name, address, phone, website, hours, service area, categories, photos, and a short business description.
- **Why:** Yelp pages rank well for "appliance repair Houston" style searches, and the listing itself is a citation.

## 4. Angi (formerly Angie's List)

- **Sign-up:** https://www.angi.com/companylist/join/
- **Suggested category:** Appliance Repair — Large.
- **Free:** a basic profile is free; leads and advertising are paid.
- **What it asks for:** business name, address, phone, website, services offered, service area ZIP codes, licence and insurance details, and in most categories a background check.
- **Why:** high-intent homeowner traffic, and the ZIP-level service area matches the way our service-area pages are built.

## 5. Houston Hispanic Chamber of Commerce (or the Houston West Chamber)

- **Sign-up:** https://www.houstonhispanicchamber.com/membership/ — alternative: https://www.houstonwestchamber.org/
- **Suggested category:** Home Services / Repair & Maintenance.
- **Free:** no. Chamber membership is an annual fee (tiered by company size); the member directory listing is included.
- **What it asks for:** legal business name, address, phone, website, owner contact, business category and a short description.
- **Why:** a local, Houston-specific, genuinely editorial directory link. Chamber domains carry real local authority, which generic directories do not, and the Houston West Chamber covers the Energy Corridor and Katy corridor we serve.

### Also worth doing, no sign-up friction

Thumbtack, Nextdoor Business and Houzz are all free to list and accept the same NAP
block. They are lower priority than the five above because the link value is smaller,
but they cost nothing beyond the time to fill the form.
