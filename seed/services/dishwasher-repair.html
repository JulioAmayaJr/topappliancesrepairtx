Common problems

Common dishwasher problems we repair

A dishwasher has to fill, heat, spray, drain and dry. When one of those steps fails, you see one of these symptoms.

Dishes come out dirty

A clogged filter, blocked spray arms, low water fill from a failing inlet valve or a weak wash pump leaves food on dishes.

Water left in the bottom

A clogged filter or drain hose, a jammed drain pump or a blocked garbage disposal connection keeps water from draining.

Leaking onto the floor

A worn door gasket, a cracked spray arm, loose hose clamps or a leaking pump seal are the common sources.

Dishes don’t dry

A failed heating element, a high-limit thermostat, an empty rinse-aid dispenser or a vent fan problem leaves everything wet.

Won’t start

A door latch switch, a control board, a blown thermal fuse or a lack of power at the outlet can keep the cycle from starting.

Bad smell

Food trapped in the filter, standing water from a slow drain or a drain hose without a high loop lets odors build up.

Loud grinding or humming

Hard debris in the pump, a failing wash motor or a worn bearing make noises that weren’t there before.

Door won’t latch

A bent latch, a failed door switch or worn door springs can stop the door from locking, and the dishwasher won’t run.

When to call

Signs your dishwasher needs a technician now

  • Water is leaking onto the floor or into the cabinet under the sink.
  • Water is still standing in the tub after the cycle ends.
  • You smell burning or see smoke from the control panel. Turn it off at the breaker.
  • The dishwasher trips the breaker or makes a loud grinding noise.
  • The door won’t latch, so the cycle never starts.

Safe checks before you call

  1. Remove and rinse the filter at the bottom of the tub; it is often the whole problem.
  2. Spin the spray arms by hand and clear any debris from their holes.
  3. If you just installed a garbage disposal, ask us about the drain knockout plug, and run the disposal before starting the dishwasher.
  4. Run the kitchen faucet until the water is hot before starting a cycle.
  5. Use fresh dishwasher detergent and refill the rinse-aid dispenser.
  6. Check that nothing on the bottom rack blocks the door or the spray arm.

Stay safe: don’t remove the panel under the door or touch the wiring. If water is leaking, stop the cycle, turn off the dishwasher and call us.

Why a dishwasher stops cleaning

Cleaning problems are rarely about detergent alone. The wash pump has to push hot water through spray arms that are free of debris, and the water has to reach the right level and temperature. A clogged filter, a weak inlet valve or a failing pump all reduce cleaning power. We check each part of that system instead of guessing, so the fix lasts.

Keep your dishwasher working

  • Clean the filter once a month, or every week if you skip pre-rinsing.
  • Scrape food into the trash instead of the dishwasher.
  • Load so that nothing blocks the spray arms or the detergent door.
  • Run a hot empty cycle with dishwasher cleaner every few months.
  • Check the door gasket for tears and wipe off food residue.
