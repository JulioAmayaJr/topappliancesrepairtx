How we work

Every job follows the same four steps, so there are no surprises.

  1. You tell us what the appliance is doing. Call or use the booking form and describe the symptom — not cooling, not draining, no heat, a code on the display. That tells the technician which parts to bring.
  2. A technician comes out, usually the same day. We confirm a time with you first. You do not need to take the appliance anywhere; all work is done at your home.
  3. You get a free estimate before any work starts. The technician finds the cause, explains it in plain language and gives you the price. Nothing is repaired until you approve it, and you are free to say no.
  4. We make the repair and stand behind it. Most repairs are finished in that first visit. The parts we install and the labor are covered by our 90-day warranty.

When replacing beats repairing, we say so

Not every appliance is worth fixing. A sealed-system failure on an old, inexpensive refrigerator, or a basic countertop microwave with a dead magnetron, can cost more to repair than to replace. Because the estimate comes first and costs nothing, you get an honest comparison instead of a repair you did not need. Built-in and panel-ready appliances are usually the opposite case: replacing one means matching a cabinet opening and trim, so a repair is almost always the better value.
