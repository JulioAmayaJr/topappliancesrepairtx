Common problems

Common garbage disposal problems we fix

Most disposal calls come down to a jam, a leak or a dead motor — and each one has a clear fix.

Hums but won’t grind

The motor has power but the plate is jammed — usually a bone fragment, fruit pit, glass or a utensil. Don’t keep pressing the switch; the motor can burn out.

Completely dead

A tripped internal overload (the red reset button), a tripped breaker or GFCI, a failed wall switch or a burned-out motor.

Leaking under the sink

Leaks at the sink flange or hose connections are repairable. A leak from the bottom of the body means failed internal seals — that unit gets replaced.

Drains slowly or backs up

Ground food packed in the discharge pipe or trap, often after grinding rice, pasta or coffee grounds, keeps the sink from clearing.

Metal rattling or grinding noise

A loose object bouncing in the chamber, a bent impeller or worn mounting parts make the disposal sound like a rock tumbler.

Bad smell from the drain

Food residue coating the chamber and splash guard. Usually cleaning solves it; a disposal that smells despite cleaning may be draining poorly.

Trips the reset or breaker repeatedly

An overloaded or failing motor, a persistent jam or a wiring fault. Repeated trips mean it needs service, not another reset.

When to call

Signs your disposal needs a technician now

  • It hums with the switch on but nothing turns.
  • Water drips from the unit body onto the cabinet floor.
  • The reset button pops again right after you press it.
  • The sink backs up every time the disposal runs.
  • Metal grinding noises that don’t stop after removing visible objects.

Safe checks before you call

  1. Turn the wall switch off before doing anything else.
  2. Press the red reset button on the bottom of the unit, then try again.
  3. Check the breaker panel and any GFCI outlet on the same circuit.
  4. With the power off, use a flashlight to look for visible objects and remove them with tongs — never fingers.
  5. Run cold water for 15 seconds before and after grinding to move waste through the pipe.

Stay safe: never put your hand into a disposal, even one that is switched off, and never run it without water. If the unit trips the breaker repeatedly, leave it off until the technician arrives.

Keep your disposal out of trouble

  • Always run cold water before, during and a few seconds after grinding.
  • Keep grease, coffee grounds, rice, pasta, bones, pits and fibrous scraps out of it.
  • Feed scraps gradually instead of packing the chamber full.
  • Grind a few ice cubes now and then to knock residue off the plate, and see our guide on fixing a smelly garbage disposal.
  • If something metal falls in, switch the unit off before fishing it out with tongs.
